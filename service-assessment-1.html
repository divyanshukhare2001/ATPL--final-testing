About The Service

One Connected Assessment Lifecycle

Our assessment services organize the operational stages that sit before, during, and after an examination into a clear, traceable workflow.

Planning covers the examination process, timelines, resources, centres, systems, supervisors, and candidate allocation. Delivery can include registration, multilingual assessment, identity verification, monitoring, and audit trails.

After the examination, we support response processing, evaluation, certification, publishing, analytics, re-evaluation workflows, and operational records.
